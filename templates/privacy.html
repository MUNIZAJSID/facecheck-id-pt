Privacy Policy

TLDR

We strive to make searches as private and confidential as possible. Uploaded photos are not indexed and are deleted after 24 hours together with cached search results. FaceCheck.ID does not log IP addresses and does not use third-party visitor tracking cookies such as Google Analytics. FaceCheck.ID does not create, store, or process biometric data used for identifying individuals.

Long Version

This privacy policy discloses the privacy practices for FaceCheck.ID. This privacy policy applies solely to information collected by this website. It will notify you of the following:


  • What personally identifiable information is collected from you through the website, how it is used and with whom it may be shared.
  • What choices are available to you regarding the use of your data.
  • The security procedures in place to protect the misuse of your information.
  • How you can correct any inaccuracies in the information.

Information Collection, Use, and Sharing

FaceCheck.ID does not collect or share any personally identifiable information about their users.

FaceCheck.ID does not log IP addresses of their visitors. Uploaded photo is used to extract a face and use the extracted face for search only. Original photo is discarded immediately after face extraction. Low resolution thumbnail of extracted face is deleted after 24 hours together with cached search results. We do not index, publish or share uploaded photos.

About FaceCheck.ID database
FaceCheck.ID indexes and caches small low-resolution cropped thumbnails and URL links that are indexed from public, readily available web pages such as public news websites and blogs. We do not store any other information such as name, location, or whether or not a person was involved in any criminal activity. FaceCheck.ID's AI is trained not to index or search children's faces.

Cookies

We use "cookies" on this site. A cookie is a piece of data stored on a site visitor's hard drive to help us improve your access to our site and identify repeat visitors to our site.

Links

This website contains links to other sites. Please be aware that we are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of such other sites. We encourage our users to be aware when they leave our site and to read the privacy statements of any other site that collects personally identifiable information.

Security

We take precautions to protect all your information. When you submit any information via the website, your information is protected both online and offline.

All information is transmitted to us in a secure way via SSL. You can verify this by looking for a lock icon in the address bar and looking for "https" at the beginning of the address of the Web page.

While we use encryption to protect sensitive information transmitted online, we also protect your information offline. We do not collect personally identifiable information.

Processing of Image Data

We provide an image-similarity search service and are committed to protecting user privacy in how image data is handled. We do not create, store, or process biometric data used for the unique identification of individuals. Our technology does not extract or generate facial biometric templates or identifiers.

Our system uses similarity analysis to compare visual features of images and find visually similar photos. The process evaluates visual patterns only and does not perform personal identification or recognition. Search results are linked solely to publicly available web pages containing the matched images. The system does not associate, infer, or connect these images with specific persons or personal identities.

Uploaded images are transformed into non-invertible visual descriptors used only for technical comparison. These descriptors cannot be used to reconstruct the original image and are retained only as needed for system functionality and security.

User Control

Users can request an instant removal of specific images or results from our database at any time through our takedown request form.
